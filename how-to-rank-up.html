How to Rank Up

How to rank up: Contribution, longevity or activity.

Contribution (60%)
When you engage in the text channels or voice chats

Longevity (30%)
When you are in the server for a longer period of time

Activity (10%)
When you are actively online

All new members start at the lowest rank
Members who possess all 3 qualities rank up the fastest
You can rank up only one at a time to enforce fairness
If you leave and rejoin you keep the rank you left with (unless if you leave for to long)
The higher rank you have, the harder it is to get promoted
Demotions may happen due to bad behavior or inactivity
