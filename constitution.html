Constitution

We are fully committed to the continuation of the reinforcement and facilitation of the Stuttering Empire to help out as many people as possible to reduce various problems and consequently lead to their stopping.

We recognize that people have different knowledge bases so they are able to contribute differently that consequently contributes to the empire. Anyone and everyone is able to make a difference here. We have already done what we were set out to accomplish. We have already figured out how to beat stuttering. We have already figured out how to beat anxiety. There are so many more problems that someone can experience that we have already solved using the brute-force + analysis approach, reverse engineering, correlation vs. causation and the means of attrition. These are primary goals that have been achieved. What follows is to satisfy an obligation to society by sharing the knowedge that we have discovered here with as many people as possible. This doesn't imply recruitment into the empire per se but to be able to explain to others how to stop these problems without giving medical advice or asking for medical history of course. Additionally being able to teach people how to problem solve to get to the right solution is important for optimization sake so that people do not limit their own progress. With that being said, we will continue to slightly refine our solutions if needed. We will continue to explore other topics of interest as we always have been to expand our knowledge base.

We assume that various neurological and psychological problems especially ones that we present are able to be fixed. We do not believe that anxiety, stuttering, OCD or depression (just to name a few examples) are genetically caused but rather environmentally caused therefore can be fixed by taking action in the environment that reshapes the brain. The reason is because limbic system firing is independent of your genetic makeup. There are vastly different genetic makeups out there but everyone has different firing of the limbic system during certain situations. The goal is to be able to reduce activity of the limbic system given a certain situation to be able to increase performance by not feeling mentally held back. We have already met this goal which is why we are passively refining the methodology if needed but we are fully confident by our solutions and approach.

The reason why people may believe that such psychological conditions are genetic is because they do not understand the malleability of the brain or because they were not successful in finding a good solution to the problem. This is what gives us more than enough confidence that these problems are able to be successfully addressed. If it is an environmentally caused problem then the solution must also be environmental while the information is being fed back to the brain to create change.

In addition, being able to produce consistent results leads to positive reinforcement which leads to changes to the belief system which leads to additional confidence in our ability to address such psychological problems. We do not give nor offer medical advice nor do we try to influence your medical decision-making.

  1. You need to change the belief system to get motivation
  2. But how do you even change the belief system? By positive reinforcement
  3. This means that you think about your past successes which reshapes belief
  4. Now you start believing you can achieve the reward (by fixing the problem)
  5. This leads to more motivation to take actions in the environment
  6. More motivation ⟶ stronger drive ⟶ more willingness to take action
  7. Then the output gets re-inputted via the feedback loop and adjusts the belief system, the motivation level and the probability of you succeeding at achieving the reward (which is again to fix the problem)
