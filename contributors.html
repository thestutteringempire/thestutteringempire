Demented

Demented is known for creating a speech technique called mouth mechanics in mid to late 2021 due to a series of experimentations on his own speech impediment. As a result of him stuttering alone he has a lot of opportunity to figure out what the cause of the block is and how to mitigate it. He founded the stuttering server an year later in October 2022 and has expanded to many more topics using his mathematics and neuroscience background such as anxiety and negative emotion reduction, OCD, trauma, depression, ADHD, insomnia and talks about additional topics that are useful to understand such as exposure therapy/CBT/ERP, meditation and correlation vs. causation. He has Youtube channels which you can find HERE and HERE.

Puppy

Puppy is a true pioneer of the stuttering community. He is known for self-experimentation trying out as many different methods as possible to try to get to the bottom of what stuttering even is and how it is caused. He continued to do trial-and-error and was able to gather useful insights. Despite stutterers struggle on particular sounds more than others, the sound itself does not ultimately matter because the solution is going to be the same for any sound it's just that the solution isn't naturally being applied to the sounds that are being struggled on and that the solution is naturally being applied to the sounds not struggled on. And this solution is of course going to be muscle memory where the brain relearns how to speak the right way. Puppy believes that stutterers tend to scan ahead and know when the hard block is going to arise so the moment that the hard block arises instead of trying to force through the word try to do a slight pause before trying to say the word which he calls the 'mental reset' which allows him to unblock himself in real-time. Puppy is more of a believer in a reactionary approach by trying to fix the problem in real-time compared to a preventative approach. He takes one step back to be able to take multiple steps forward is the analogy I would use to describe his method. This means that it doesn't matter who is stuttering as long as they can have a slight pause before the word they struggle on they are able to say it fluently. In addition Puppy used to conduct an experiment with an unnamed individual who had a severe stutter where Puppy asked him to repeat the same sentence over and over again to the point where the individual was able to say the sentence completely fluently which is more evidence of the brain learning how to say the sentence properly after a lot of tries because messing up/saying things disfluently is expected and is a part of the learning process rather than an inherent defective process.

He has a Youtube channel where he used to upload stuttering videos but they were eventually privatized so now it is predominantly a gaming channel which you can find HERE.

Craig Coffee

Craig Coffee is known for his charisma, ability to communicate and strong eye contact. His ability to present information clearly and concisely is hard to beat. He is known for making walking tours while turning the camera onto himself having a conversation not only into the camera but through the camera speaking directly to his audience. He is particularly known for the mall video and the Starbucks videos as he is ordering coffee. He has shown high level confidence by being able to share his experiences with how he was able to significantly reduce his stuttering and how he has 20 years of experience doing so. He has such high confidence that he can record people and himself without feeling social pressure. He used to host events on the server and took various questions about various topics including stuttering, anxiety, and trauma and how to overcome these problems. Check out his channel HERE.

Ibz

Ibz is one of the most confident people because he has different problems happening at the same time such as stuttering, anxiety and facial tics and can still video chat with others and has even participated in a long 40-minute interview which is more than impressive which is not something most people would be able to do. His primary method is Zen meditation where he focuses on the breath to ground himself to the present moment which allows him to focus that much less on the past and future intrusive thoughts and to focus more on what's currently happening. He does this to shift attention from the ruminatory thoughts and back onto the current state of mind which allows him to worry about things that much less and understands the impermanence of things. He has a channel HERE where he talks about different topics.
