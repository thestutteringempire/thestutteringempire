Our History

We were founded by Demented on October 15, 2022 as the Stuttering Empire where we branched off from the Stuttering Support Group due to reasons of censorship and mass banning. We needed to make it mission to help as many people as possible when it came to stuttering because what we were doing was much more effective compared to what others are doing. This led to jealousy amongst members of SSG which led to our breakaway. Ever since our founding, we have been active, consistent and determined to achieve the mission to gain as much relevant knowledge as possible when it comes to stopping stuttering while being able to produce effective results. I founded a speech technique called Mouth Mechanics back in 2021 where it helped me go from a severe hard blocker to much more fluent, nowadays I am around 98% fluent to the point where it goes undetected. Because I stutter alone, I have had an insane amount of opportunities to study myself becoming the most extensive stuttering case study where I studied over ~40,000 speech blocks trying to figure out what the cause of stuttering is. I realized that the mouth positioning is off in relation to the timing of the brain's intent to speak. This is where people from SSG took issue with calling it an unproven or unorthodox method which accelerated the breaking away from SSG. Not to mention there's a lot of defeatist mentality there because they were unable to fix their own speech impediments and use the coping mechanism by saying that stuttering is genetic and there's nothing you can do other than accept that you have stuttering. We greatly disagree with this coping mechanism where stuttering is entirely learned and not genetic as it is impossible for a technique to be able to override/change your current genetic makeup to the point where you go almost entirely fluent. In addition I was able to amass 150 subscribers in just 2 videos where those two videos in total got 10,000+ views, although those have since been private because I value serious content over entertaining content (more academic videos explaining different topics related to stuttering over stuttering in front of people online to show that stuttering isn't so scary). From the beginning we have had the originals such as Furqan, Book and RJ that have stayed with us for years. Ibz joined shortly after and then more notable members such as Craig Coffee. We continued to grow in numbers getting even more notable members such as Samwill and Ahmed who often spread the word of Abrahamic religion as well as the mysterious force that is Javier. We continue to grow because our mission is to help as many people as humanly possible to do humanity a service. It is much more efficient to address the cause of stuttering directly to get much more efficient results compared to focusing on correlations to get passive results. This was a big step because there are so many correlations out there when it comes to stuttering that I removed them one by one to figure out the direct cause. Stutterers tend to take the wrong approach which is why they have inconsistent results since they try to stop stuttering by focusing on a correlation. This is why a stutterer can work for years and they still don't get anywhere or they barely get anywhere. They are too focused on a byproduct of a hard block for example or the hard block itself when the focus should be shifted to before the hard block because the cause comes before the hard block. So when someone tries to fix their continuous exhalation, they don't realize that the hard block is what causes a disruption of the exhalation so fixing the hard block stops the disruption of the exhalation not trying to fix the disruption of the exhalation to try to stop the hard block the swaps get swapped thus leading to barely any progress. I'm sure you understand by this point as to why it is important for us to figure out how to stop stuttering and the amount of effort that it took to get here. Not to mention the number of benefits is too much such as improved social life, dating life, public speaking skills, higher chances of getting hired and so on. Communication skills is one of the most important skills that you can have and stuttering is able to force you into introversion if you aren't able to speak. Also not to mention stuttering can lead to getting socially rejected, feeling embarrassed, feeling anxious, can induce panic attacks, can even make you feel depressed. All of these are motivating reasons to fixing your stuttering. But then we realized something, we had already accomplished the original goal of figuring everything out about what both stuttering and selective mutism are and how to stop these speech impediments. There were other primary goals that we wanted to address. Simultaneously to me self-experimenting to figure out how to stop stuttering, I was also engaging in both supervised and unsupervised neuroscience research try to address other big problems that someone can have such as fear (before a hypothetical bad outcome), negative emotions (after a bad outcome), depression (losing hope/meaning in life, seemingly impossible to prevent a bad outcome), OCD (having to perform a ritual to avoid a bad outcome), and so on. There are many other psychological problems that someone can have that we address but we of course never give medical advice we do not try to change or influence your decision-making on the matter. Because the theory and applications for these various psychological problems have been figured out and documented, we do not always talk about just these topics. Throughout our history we engage in productive discussions when it comes to academic subjects such as the philosophy of mind and what the nature of reality is, the monotheistic religions and so on. We also engage in debating to get our views challenged so that we either strengthen our views, readjust or even change views. We learn to debate without bias by seeking the truth as the main desire that drives the conversation rather than trying to use evidence to argue to try to defend something you want to be true.
