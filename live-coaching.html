Sample of some things FLUENTling covers and the approach to his program:

the biokybernetic approach states that the distinct systems that create fluent speech together are not inherently sick but their coordination needs certain thresholds for certain parameters to produce fluent speech and these thresholds are not met by the stutterer so the output gets defective.
that's why it is aligning these systems with each other by giving input that leads to these parameters being met and to facilitate this pattern through repetition
there you have to find away we're u make steady practice in a frequency that disables anxiety to spike back up to its former levels but instead u need to continuously chisel away at the anxiety amount in certain situations through repeated voluntary exposure
Which is why my training is holistically oriented and they always get homework to work on
Although it's important to give the right task for this exposure also. In the beginning it's just about going out there and later also about focusing in certain things speechwise
I can't ask someone who just started out with training 2 weeks ago to focus on x as he is not even able to replicate x alone as the pattern is not hardened yet
it has to be accompanied with the articulation exercises so they know what to focus on in those situations when u got your patterns under control
it's the mistiming not the fear as a root cause
if the anxiety is insanely high this will not work , if people hyperventilate and can't themselves down at a certain threshold that does not work
In those cases my former speech trainer would together with a psychologist who was helping the stutterers to bear the speaking situation and then the training continued
Like if someone came to me and said he had depressive thoughts in speaking situations due to their stutter I would also refer them to a therapist first , as I don't feel equipped to deal with existential anxiety of that level and dealing with depressive people is really dangerous if u don't know exactly what u are doing.
So I would just say : hey this approach would do x with the target y
we have neuroplasticity. So what we are doing with the components changes the command center that signals to those components what to do
I was a really quick talker and reader and just overwhelmed my matters with shallow breath and too quick speech for my articulations to keep up with
If you have a disposition to develop a stutter the symptoms often stem from a lack of deep breath to support articulation while exhaling - at some point your body tries to support what is demanded and then starts pressing sounds out as the breath is not there
That's also how my stutter started
It is the connection of breath and articulation as it is not rhythmisized
